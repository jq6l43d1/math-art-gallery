Apollonian Gasket

Circle packing, 2026. Python and numpy.
k4 = 2 (k1 + k2 + k3) - k4_old
k4 z4 = 2 (k1 z1 + k2 z2 + k3 z3) - k4_old z4_old

Fill a circle with three touching circles, then fill every curved triangular gap with the largest circle that fits, forever. Descartes worked out the size of each new circle in 1643; a later complex-number version also gives its position. The smaller the circle, the brighter and warmer its color.

Around 200 BC Apollonius of Perga asked how to draw a circle tangent to three given circles. Repeating that construction in every gap gives the gasket. In 1643 Rene Descartes found a simple relation between the curvatures of four mutually tangent circles, and in 2002 it was extended to complex numbers so that it gives the centers too.

Starting from three equal circles inside a unit circle, every circle here comes from applying that relation over and over until the circles are smaller than a pixel, about 4,500 in all. The dust left between them is a fractal with a dimension of about 1.31.
